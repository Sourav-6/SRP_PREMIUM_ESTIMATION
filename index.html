Premium Calculation Guide

1. Base Premium & Chronic Loading

Based on age and Sum Insured. An additional +25% Chronic Loading is applied directly to the individual base premium of any member with ABCD chronic conditions.

2. Floater Discount

The oldest member is treated as Primary (100% premium). All subsequent members receive a 55% discount on their base premium.

3. Deductible Discount

Reduces premium if you choose to pay initial claims out of pocket. Discount percentages (up to 90%) are tiered by Sum Insured (<25L, =25L, >25L).

4. NRI & Lifetime Discounts

NRI Discount: 40% discount if all members are NRIs.
Lifetime Discount: 5% discount if all members are 35 or less.

5. Existing Customer Discount

Existing HDFC Ergo Customer: 2.5% discount for active customers.

6. Claim Loading

A +20% loading (increase) is applied if a claim was made in the last 2 years.

7. Favourable Claims Discount

Applied year-by-year based on claims history. Only applies to members under 60 years old on their sequential premium base.

8. Long Term Discount

Applied at the end of the calculation sequence: 6% discount for 2-year tenure, or 8% discount for 3 to 5 years tenure.

Premium Calculation & Discount Guide

This guide details how base rates, floater discounts, rider surcharges, and long-term tenure discounts are calculated.

1. Member Base Rates & Floater Discounts

  • Primary Member (Self / Eldest): 100% of applicable base rate for age band.
  • Spouse / 2nd Adult: -55% Floater Discount (pays 45% of base rate).
  • Children (up to 4): -50% to -65% Floater Discount per child depending on plan.

2. Deductible Discounts

  • Voluntary deductible choices (e.g. ₹10k, ₹25k, ₹50k, ₹1L - ₹25L).
  • Saves up to -40% to -65% on total base premium depending on Sum Insured and Deductible selected.

3. Policy History & Loyalty Discounts

  • Existing HDFC Ergo Customer: -5% Loyalty Discount
  • Claim-Free History: Up to -10% Favourable Claims Discount for policy continuity without claims.

4. Rider Surcharges (% of Base)

  • Unlimited Restore Rider: +2.5% to +3% of Base Premium
  • Limitless Rider: +12.5% of Base Premium
  • Optima Wellbeing Rider: +6.0% of Base Premium

5. Long-Term Tenure Discounts

  • 2 Years Tenure: -7.5% to -8.0% Discount on annual premium
  • 3 Years Tenure: -8.0% to -10.0% Discount on annual premium
  • 4 & 5 Years Tenure: -10.0% to -12.0% Discount on annual premium

6. GST

Standard mandatory 18% GST is added to the net premium after all discounts and riders.
