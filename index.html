Premium Calculation Guide

1. Base Premium & Chronic Loading

Based on age and Sum Insured. An additional +25% Chronic Loading is applied directly to the individual base premium of any member with ABCD chronic conditions.

2. Floater Discount

The oldest member is treated as Primary (100% premium). All subsequent members receive a 55% discount on their base premium.

3. Deductible Discount

Reduces premium if you choose to pay initial claims out of pocket. Discount percentages (up to 90%) are tiered by Sum Insured (<25L, =25L, >25L).

4. NRI & Lifetime Discounts

NRI Discount: 40% discount if all members are NRIs.
Lifetime Discount: 5% discount for new buyers if all members are under 35.

5. Porting & Existing Customer

Porting Discount: 5% discount for switching policies.
Existing HDFC Ergo Customer: 8% discount for active customers.

6. Claim Loading

A +20% loading (increase) is applied if a claim was made in the last 2 years.

7. Favourable Claims Discount

Applied year-by-year based on claims history. Only applies to members under 60 years old on their sequential premium base.

8. Long Term Discount

Applied at the end of the calculation sequence: 6% discount for 2-year tenure, or 8% discount for 3 to 5 years tenure.
